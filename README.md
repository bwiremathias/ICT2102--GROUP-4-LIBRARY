🌍 1. About Knowledge Hub Library

Knowledge Hub Library is a web-based library information platform developed as part of the ICT 2102 – Web Development Group Practical Assignment.

The platform is designed to connect students, teachers, researchers and community members with educational information, reading materials and library services.

It provides a welcoming digital environment where users can explore learning resources, discover books, register for membership, request books and share feedback.

Our vision is to make knowledge accessible to everyone through technology.

🎯 2. Project Objectives

- 📖 Promote a culture of reading and lifelong learning.
- 🎓 Support students and teachers in academic development.
- 🔍 Encourage research and independent learning.
- 💻 Promote digital literacy and access to educational resources.
- 🤝 Provide an inclusive learning environment for the community.
- 🌱 Encourage knowledge sharing and innovation.
