How to Borrow a Book

  1. Search for the book you need in the library.
  2. Take the book to the library circulation desk.
  3. Present your valid student or library identification.
  4. Register the book with the librarian.
  5. Return the book on or before the due date.

Library Rules

Important: Keep silence inside the library to maintain a good study environment for everyone.

  • Keep library books clean and in good condition.
  • Return borrowed books on time.
  • Do not damage or remove pages from books.
  • Keep mobile phones on silent mode.
  • Respect other library users.

Why Use Our Library?

Our library provides a comfortable environment where learners can concentrate, conduct research, and improve their knowledge. Learning never stops when you have access to good resources.
